Health tips

Small habits, big health.

💧

Take medicines with water

Unless your pharmacist says otherwise, swallow tablets with a full glass of plain water - not juice, tea or soft drinks, which can affect how some medicines work.

🌡️

Store medicines right

Keep medicines in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and out of children's reach. Bathroom cabinets are often too humid - a bedroom drawer is usually better.

📅

Finish your course

Feeling better doesn't mean the job is done. Always complete the full course your doctor or pharmacist prescribed, especially antibiotics.
